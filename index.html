300 SL · 1955

300 SL "Gullwing"

1954 – 1957

The car that invented the upward-opening door out of necessity, not theatre — its tubular racing frame left no room for conventional sills. The first production car with direct fuel injection, and still the yardstick every modern gullwing is measured against. Roughly 1,400 coupes were built before Mercedes moved on.

M1 · 1979

BMW M1

1978 – 1981

BMW's only mid-engine production car for four decades, born from a shelved racing programme with Lamborghini. The Procar series built around it briefly put Formula 1 drivers behind the wheel of a showroom-legal machine. Fewer than 460 were ever completed.
